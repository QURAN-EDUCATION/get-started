About Quran Education (QE)

A Dynamic Ecosystem for Faith & Skill Development

Quran Education (QE) is an international non-profit entity dedicated to the holistic transformation of the Muslim Ummah. We sit at the intersection of modern software engineering institutions, tech enterprises, and classical Islamic learning centers (Madaris, Mosques, and Community Foundations).

Vision

Building the world's largest collaborative network of tech developers, scholars, and youth spreading the authentic Quran & Sunnah in native languages.

Mission

Bridging academia, industry, and Islamic religious institutions through mutual skill exchange, open-source technology, and authentic ethical guidance.
